# Phase 4 — Product Vision Model

> Part of the sereneAI.CCOS Phase 4 deliverable set. Baseline: [PROJECT_BIBLE.md](../PROJECT_BIBLE.md).

## 1. Product purpose

Eliminate operational fragmentation in contact centres by giving every role — agent to
executive — one coherent operational workspace that connects the systems they already use,
the knowledge they rely on, and the work they own.

## 2. Product vision

sereneAI.CCOS becomes the **operating system for modern contact centres**: the layer through
which daily operations, knowledge, work, reporting, integrations and AI assistance are run.
Users start their day in CCOS and spend the majority of it there — not because they are
forced to, but because everything they need is one click away and trustworthy.

## 3. Vision statement

> **One workspace. Every system. All your work.**
> sereneAI.CCOS unifies the fragmented contact centre — integrating the tools you have,
> replacing them only when you're ready, and building the trusted operational foundation
> that makes AI genuinely useful.

## 4. Elevator pitch

For contact centre leaders drowning in disconnected systems, sereneAI.CCOS is a Contact
Centre Operating System that sits above your existing telephony, CRM, knowledge and task
tools and gives every employee one place to find their work, their knowledge and their
numbers. Unlike intranets, dashboards or bolt-on chatbots, CCOS integrates first, replaces
second, automates third and transforms with AI fourth — so you get value in weeks and an
AI-ready operation, not another silo.

## 5. Value proposition by audience

| Audience | Value |
|---|---|
| Agents | Start of shift to end of shift in one workspace: my tasks, my knowledge, my tools, my announcements — no more eight open tabs |
| Team Leaders | See and direct the team's work, push knowledge and comms, escalate and follow up with clear ownership |
| Operations Managers | A unified operational picture; evidence of what knowledge is used, what work is stuck, where risk is building |
| Executives | Operational visibility and AI adoption on a governed, auditable foundation |
| IT / CIO | One integration layer instead of N point solutions; RBAC+ABAC, tenant isolation, audit logging, SSO-ready; no rip-and-replace |
| Compliance | Versioned knowledge with lifecycle governance, read-acknowledgement evidence, immutable audit trail, governed AI |

## 6. Strategic goals

1. **Prove the wedge (MVP):** one workspace that measurably reduces tool switching for a
   pilot contact centre within 90 days of deployment.
2. **Earn the habit (Release 1):** Home + My Work + Knowledge become the default first tab
   of the working day for >70% of pilot users.
3. **Demonstrate governed AI (Release 2):** source-backed, permission-aware Knowledge Q&A
   that buyers can audit end-to-end.
4. **Open the Replace path (V1):** first customer switches Planner off in favour of
   sereneAI Tasks.
5. **Stay one platform:** every customer on the shared, upgradeable core; zero forked deployments.

## 7. User outcomes

- I know what I need to do today without asking anyone.
- I find the correct, current policy/procedure in under 30 seconds.
- I reach every system I need from one place.
- I see what matters to my role the moment I log in.
- When I ask the assistant, I get an answer with sources I can check.

## 8. Business outcomes

- Reduced average handling friction (fewer tool switches per interaction).
- Faster onboarding of new agents (knowledge + tasks + links in one place).
- Reduced operational risk (governed knowledge, audit evidence, acknowledged comms).
- A trusted data/knowledge foundation that makes later AI capabilities credible.
- Recurring revenue per seat with expansion via native modules and AI tier.

## 9. Success metrics (headline)

Full framework: [10-product-success-framework.md](./10-product-success-framework.md).

- Weekly active users / licensed seats ≥ 80% by pilot day 60.
- Knowledge search success rate ≥ 85% (search → open within session).
- Task throughput visible in CCOS for ≥ 90% of team-leader-assigned work.
- Assistant answer usefulness (thumbs-up rate) ≥ 70% with citation present on 100% of answers.
- Tool-switching proxy: ≥ 50% of pilot users launch external systems via CCOS quick links.

## 10. What this product is not

Not an intranet, portal, dashboard, chatbot, CRM, telephony, WFM, QA or ticketing tool —
and the MVP must never be reducible to one of these in a demo. Every demo narrative leads
with the *operating system* story: integrate, unify, govern, then transform.
